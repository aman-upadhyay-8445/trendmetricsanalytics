Sales Dashboard

Real-Time Sales Performance Dashboard

The Problem

A B2B SaaS company with a 15-person sales team was spending 4+ hours every Monday manually compiling sales reports in Excel. The data was always outdated, and the sales manager couldn't see real-time pipeline health or identify at-risk deals until it was too late.

  • • Manual weekly reporting taking 4+ hours
  • • No visibility into pipeline health
  • • Deals slipping through cracks
  • • Inability to forecast accurately

The Solution

We built an automated sales dashboard that pulls directly from their CRM (HubSpot) and refreshes every hour. The dashboard shows real-time pipeline value, deal stage distribution, win rates by rep, and automated forecasting.

Marketing Funnel

Marketing Funnel Analytics Dashboard

The Problem

An e-commerce startup was spending $50K/month on marketing across 8 channels but had no clear view of which channels were actually driving revenue. They couldn't calculate true CAC or ROI, and were making budget decisions based on gut feel rather than data.

  • • No visibility into channel performance
  • • Couldn't calculate true CAC or ROI
  • • Budget allocation based on guesswork
  • • No funnel conversion tracking

The Solution

We built a comprehensive marketing funnel dashboard that tracks leads from first touchpoint through conversion. Integrated Google Analytics, Facebook Ads, email marketing, and Shopify data to show true attribution and ROI by channel.

  • • Multi-channel attribution tracking
  • • Automated CAC & ROI calculations
  • • Funnel conversion analysis
  • • Real-time budget performance

Operations Performance Dashboard

The Problem

  • • Data scattered across multiple systems
  • • No real-time operational visibility
  • • Slow response to performance issues
  • • Inefficient resource allocation

The Solution

We created a unified operations dashboard that aggregates data from Zendesk, their internal database, and time-tracking tools. The dashboard shows real-time metrics on ticket volume, resolution times, team performance, and operational efficiency.

  • • Unified data from all systems

Finance & Profitability

Finance & Profitability Dashboard

The Problem

A growing SaaS company was closing their books monthly, meaning the founder only saw profitability data 30 days after the fact. They couldn't track burn rate, runway, or unit economics in real-time, making it impossible to make timely financial decisions.

  • • Monthly financial close (30-day delay)
  • • No real-time burn rate visibility
  • • Couldn't track unit economics
  • • Runway calculations were always outdated

The Solution

We built a finance dashboard that connects to QuickBooks, Stripe, and their bank accounts to show real-time revenue, expenses, profitability, burn rate, and runway. The dashboard updates daily and includes automated unit economics calculations.

  • • Real-time financial data aggregation
  • • Daily burn rate & runway calculations
  • • Automated unit economics tracking
  • • Profitability by product/segment

Runway calculations are always accurate, enabling better fundraising timing.
